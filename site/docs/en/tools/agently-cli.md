# agently-cli (Agent Mail CLI)

<ToolMeta id="agently-cli" />

An official command-line tool launched by Tencent QQ Mail, suitable for letting the Bot send and receive emails, search email history, and download attachments on the **Cloud PC**.
The Bot sends and receives emails, searches history, and downloads attachments in the Cloud PC.
Conclusion: The Bot can independently handle daily email sending and receiving, search history, and automatically download attachments on the Cloud PC.


## Prompt to Send to the Bot

Copy the entire text below and send it to your Grok Bot. It will automatically install and verify it on the Cloud PC, and will pause to wait for your action when encountering login or key configuration.

<div class="tool-prompt">

````text
帮我在你的云电脑上安装 agently-cli（https://agent.qq.com），以后收发邮件、检索历史邮件和下载附件用它。需要登录或授权时停下来把链接发给我由我完成。
````

</div>

## What You Need to Do

- Prepare a QQ Mail or Agent Mail account.
- Open the authorization link sent by the Bot in your browser to complete verification.
