# Feishu CLI (lark-cli)

<ToolMeta id="lark-cli" />

The official Feishu command-line tool, suitable for letting the Bot operate Feishu Docs, Base, Drive, and Wiki on the **cloud computer** under a personal identity.
The Bot operates Feishu Docs, Base, Drive, and Wiki on the cloud computer under a personal identity.
Suitable for users who need the Bot to manage Feishu documents, sheets, and cloud drive assets on the cloud computer under a personal identity.


## Prompt for the Bot

Copy the entire text below and send it to your Grok Bot. It will automatically install and verify it on the cloud computer, pausing to wait for your action when login or credential configuration is required.

<div class="tool-prompt">

````text
帮我在你的云电脑上安装配置飞书 CLI（https://github.com/larksuite/cli），以后操作飞书云文档、多维表格和云盘用它。需要登录或授权时停下来把链接发给我由我完成。
````

</div>

## What You Need to Do Yourself

- Prepare a personal Feishu account.
- Open the verification link, enter the device code provided by the terminal, and confirm authorization.
