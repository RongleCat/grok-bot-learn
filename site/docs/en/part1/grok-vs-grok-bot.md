# What Is the Difference Between Grok and Grok Bot

**Grok is a conversational assistant, while Grok Bot is an always-on AI teammate that works for you on a cloud computer.**
Grok handles standard Q&A—close the webpage or app and the conversation ends; Grok Bot is resident on a standalone cloud computer, so background tasks keep running even after you close the client.
Both come from xAI (now known as SpaceXSI), but they differ completely in interaction form and application positioning.

## What Is the Core Difference Between Grok and Grok Bot?

**The core difference is that Grok only talks and gives suggestions, whereas Grok Bot takes action and operates inside a cloud computer.**
Grok is purely a conversational assistant that answers when you ask; Grok Bot is an always-on AI teammate launched by xAI and Cursor on August 11, 2026, and is currently still in Beta.
To build an overall understanding, you can first read [Chapter 1: What Is Grok Bot](/part1/01-what-is).

| Dimension | Grok | Grok Bot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | xAI's AI conversational assistant | A suite of always-on AI teammates, currently in Beta |
| Where to use | grok.com web, iOS and Android apps, Grok tab in X | macOS, Windows, Linux desktop; iPhone, iPad, Android mobile |
| How it works | Answers when you ask; supports text, voice, file analysis uploads, image/video generation, etc. | Assign tasks via text or voice messages; works across applications on an always-on cloud computer equipped with a browser, filesystem, and terminal; only asks you when approval is needed |
| Runs after closing? | No; close the web page or app and the conversation ends | Yes; close your laptop or exit the app, and background tasks and routines keep running |
| Can operate websites & apps? | No; does not take action for you in external websites or software | Yes; logs into commonly used websites and applications to complete tasks; sensitive steps can be taken over by clicking Take over |
| Required subscription tier | Free tier available to try, subscriptions increase limits; included in The X Subscription Free tier, Plus tier and above | Included in The X Subscription Premium, Super, and Ultra tiers; not included in Plus |
| Best suited for | Instant Q&A, troubleshooting errors, drafting text, generating images/videos, checking latest updates on X | Fixed-process, high-frequency repetitive tasks across multiple websites or apps (e.g., website inspections, weekly reconciliation, daily morning digests, reproducing issues) |

**All Bots under the same account share a single cloud computer, sharing files, browser sessions, and app logins.**
Although each Bot has its own screen, the official documentation cautions against treating different Bots as security isolation boundaries. For details on how it works, see [Chapter 5: Shared Cloud Computer](/part1/05-computer).
To set up your environment, refer to [Chapter 3: Installation, Login, and Your First Bot](/part1/03-install).

## When to Use Grok vs. Grok Bot?

**Use Grok for instant Q&A that yields results in seconds; use Grok Bot for repetitive workflows requiring cross-app operations, scheduled runs, or multi-step execution.**

**Scenarios suitable for Grok focus on instant Q&A and one-off content generation.**
Examples include troubleshooting errors, writing a piece of text, generating images or videos (up to 15 seconds), or searching the web and X in real time for latest updates.
Such tasks take only seconds to complete and do not require spinning up a cloud computer.

**Scenarios suitable for Grok Bot primarily involve tedious tasks with fixed procedures, high-frequency repetition, and cross-website or cross-application operations.**
Typical tasks include regularly inspecting official websites to generate summaries, weekly reconciliations, daily morning report digests, reading issues to reproduce bugs, etc.
Such work must be verifiable by humans; high-risk actions (sending emails, making payments, deletions) will pause to await approval, and you can click Take over for manual intervention when encountering passwords, two-factor authentication, CAPTCHAs, or payments.

A successfully completed workflow can be saved as a skill and configured as a routine to run automatically on a schedule; multiple Bots can also collaborate and hand off tasks in group chats.
**Note that tasks such as high-frequency polling and video processing will rapidly consume your weekly quota.**
For quota planning and consumption details, it is recommended to review [Chapter 10: Quota and Costs](/part1/10-usage) in advance.

## Which Subscription Tier Is Required for Grok Bot?

**Using Grok Bot requires at least the $30/month Premium plan; the $8 Plus tier does not include Grok Bot.**

Starting in October 2026, the official unified subscription plan—The X Subscription—was introduced, consolidating Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium together.
The subscription is divided into five tiers: Free ($0), Plus ($8/month), Premium ($30/month), Super ($100/month), and Ultra ($200/month).

**Grok is available on Free and Plus tiers, whereas Grok Bot is only available on Premium, Super, and Ultra.**
Paid tiers provide a weekly shared quota pool used jointly by Grok, Grok Bot, and Grok models inside Cursor.

**Existing users can also use Grok Bot directly.**
Accounts with legacy Cursor paid personal plans, Cursor team plans, or those linked with SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, and SuperGrok Heavy can all use Grok Bot directly.
